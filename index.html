A few common questions

The details, made clear.

Every move is a little different. Here are answers to some common questions.

Where do you serve?

Lansing is the home base, with licensed and insured moving services available throughout Michigan.

How are arrival and drive times charged?

Arrival is free for homes within 25 miles of Lansing. Outside the Greater Lansing area, the listed fee is $0.76 per mile to arrive and return. The company says travel to the starting home and back to the office is not billed. For multiple trips between addresses, its stated addendum caps that drive time at 1.5 times. Ask for the exact travel calculation with your estimate.

What does the low-income discount require?

Provide proof of household tax return or unemployment. Eligible households receive $5/hour off within 200% of the federal poverty level, or $10/hour off within the federal poverty level.

Can I bring my own rental truck?

Yes. The rental discount applies whether you provide the rental or we arrange it. The $20–$50 discount applies to a larger rental; rental-provider mileage and fees are separate.

What do packing supplies cost?

Packing paper, careful handling, plastic wrap, and some donated boxes are free when available. Listed supply prices include bags at $0.50, small boxes at $2, medium boxes at $2.50, large boxes at $3, and a roll of tape at $5. Tub deposits are $2 for 40-quart tubs and $3 for 70-quart tubs, refunded when returned.

Can you help on short notice?

Moves needed within 48 hours add $10/hour; moves needed within 24 hours add $20/hour. Give us a call to talk through timing.
